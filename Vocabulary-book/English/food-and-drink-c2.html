📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
gourmand
/ˈɡʊə.mɒnd/		nouna person who loves eating, sometimes excessivelyHe's a gourmand who always orders too much.
gustatory
/ˈɡʌs.tə.tər.i/		nounrelating to the sense of tasteThe dish provided an intense gustatory experience.
tantalizing
/ˈtæn.tə.laɪ.zɪŋ/		nounextremely tempting or appealingThe tantalizing smell drew everyone to the kitchen.
pungent
/ˈpʌn.dʒənt/		nounhaving a very strong, sharp smell or tasteThe pungent smell of garlic filled the room.
insipid
/ɪnˈsɪp.ɪd/		nounlacking flavour or interestThe soup was watery and insipid.
victuals
/ˈvɪt.əlz/		nounfood or provisionsThey stocked up on victuals for the journey.
larder
/ˈlɑː.dər/		nouna room or cupboard for storing foodThe larder was full of preserves and jars.
provender
/ˈprɒv.ɪn.dər/		nounfood, especially stored supplies or animal feedThe farm kept provender for the winter.
glut
/ɡlʌt/		nounan excessive supply of somethingThere was a glut of apples this year.
hors d'oeuvre
/ɔːˈdɜːv/		nouna small dish served before the main mealThey served hors d'oeuvres before dinner.
comestible
/kəˈmes.tɪ.bəl/		nounfit to be eatenThe market sold a wide variety of comestibles.
epicurean
/ˌep.ɪ.kjʊəˈriː.ən/		noundevoted to sensual, especially culinary, pleasureTheir trip to France was an epicurean adventure.
gastronome
/ˈɡæs.trə.nəʊm/		nouna person with expert knowledge of food and drinkThe restaurant was recommended by a famous gastronome.
sate
/seɪt/		nounto satisfy a desire or appetite fullyOne slice was enough to sate his hunger.
surfeit
/ˈsɜː.fɪt/		nounan excessive amount of somethingA surfeit of rich food made him feel ill.
savour
/ˈseɪ.vər/		nounto enjoy food slowly, appreciating its tasteShe savoured every bite of the cake.
