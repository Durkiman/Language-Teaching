📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
boil
/bɔɪl/		nounto cook in very hot, bubbling waterBoil the pasta for ten minutes.
fry
/fraɪ/		nounto cook in hot oilHe fried the eggs in butter.
grill
/ɡrɪl/		nounto cook using direct heat from below or aboveWe grilled chicken for the barbecue.
roast
/rəʊst/		nounto cook food in an oven with dry heatMum is roasting a chicken for Sunday lunch.
marinate
/ˈmær.ɪ.neɪt/		nounto leave food in a sauce before cookingMarinate the meat overnight for more flavour.
season
/ˈsiː.zən/		nounto add salt, pepper, or spicesSeason the soup with salt and pepper.
leftovers
/ˈleftˌəʊ.vəz/		nounfood that remains after a mealWe had the leftovers for lunch the next day.
portion
/ˈpɔː.ʃən/		nounthe amount of food served to one personThat's a big portion for one person!
diet
/ˈdaɪ.ət/		nounthe food someone usually eatsA healthy diet includes lots of vegetables.
organic
/ɔːˈɡæn.ɪk/		noungrown without artificial chemicalsWe buy organic fruit when we can.
homemade
/ˌhəʊmˈmeɪd/		nounmade at home, not boughtThis is homemade bread — I baked it myself.
takeaway
/ˈteɪk.ə.weɪ/		nouna meal bought at a restaurant to eat elsewhereLet's order a takeaway tonight.
starter
/ˈstɑː.tər/		nounthe first small dish of a mealI'll have soup as a starter.
main course
/meɪn kɔːs/		nounthe biggest dish of a mealFor the main course, I chose the fish.
stale
/steɪl/		nounno longer freshThe bread has gone stale.
stir-fry
/ˈstɜː.fraɪ/		nounto cook quickly in a little oil while stirringStir-fry the vegetables for five minutes.
