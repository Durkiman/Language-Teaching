📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
menu
/ˈmen.juː/		nouna list of food available in a restaurantCan I see the menu, please?
waiter
/ˈweɪ.tər/		nouna person who serves food in a restaurantThe waiter brought our drinks quickly.
order
/ˈɔː.dər/		nounto ask for food or drink in a restaurantWe ordered pizza and salad.
bill
/bɪl/		nounthe piece of paper showing how much you must payCan we have the bill, please?
recipe
/ˈres.ɪ.pi/		nouninstructions for cooking a dishShe followed the recipe carefully.
ingredient
/ɪnˈɡriː.di.ənt/		nounone part of a food mixtureFlour is the main ingredient in bread.
snack
/snæk/		nouna small amount of food eaten between mealsI had a snack before dinner.
dessert
/dɪˈzɜːt/		nouna sweet dish eaten at the end of a mealWe shared a chocolate dessert.
breakfast
/ˈbrek.fəst/		nounthe first meal of the dayI never skip breakfast.
lunch
/lʌntʃ/		nounthe meal eaten in the middle of the dayLet's have lunch together.
dinner
/ˈdɪn.ər/		nounthe main meal, usually in the eveningDinner is ready at seven.
restaurant
/ˈres.tər.ɒnt/		nouna place where you pay to eat a mealWe booked a table at a new restaurant.
delicious
/dɪˈlɪʃ.əs/		nountasting very goodThis soup is delicious.
fresh
/freʃ/		nounrecently made or picked, not oldThe bread here is always fresh.
spicy
/ˈspaɪ.si/		nouncontaining strong, hot spicesI love spicy food.
sweet
/swiːt/		nountasting like sugarThe cake is too sweet for me.
