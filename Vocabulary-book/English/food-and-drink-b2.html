📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
cuisine
/kwɪˈziːn/		nouna style of cookingI love Italian cuisine.
garnish
/ˈɡɑː.nɪʃ/		nouna small decoration added to foodThe chef added a garnish of fresh herbs.
texture
/ˈteks.tʃər/		nounhow food feels in your mouthThe cake has a light, fluffy texture.
aroma
/əˈrəʊ.mə/		nouna pleasant, strong smellThe aroma of fresh coffee filled the kitchen.
staple
/ˈsteɪ.pəl/		nouna basic food eaten regularlyRice is a staple in many countries.
nutritious
/njuːˈtrɪʃ.əs/		nouncontaining substances the body needsVegetables are nutritious and low in fat.
processed
/ˈprəʊ.sest/		nounchanged a lot before being sold as foodTry to eat less processed food.
additive
/ˈæd.ɪ.tɪv/		nouna substance added to food to preserve or flavour itThis juice has no artificial additives.
craving
/ˈkreɪ.vɪŋ/		nouna strong desire for a particular foodI have a craving for chocolate.
indulge
/ɪnˈdʌldʒ/		nounto allow yourself to enjoy somethingIt's okay to indulge occasionally.
bland
/blænd/		nounhaving little or no flavourThe soup tasted a bit bland.
savoury
/ˈseɪ.vər.i/		nounsalty or spicy, not sweetI prefer savoury snacks to sweet ones.
tender
/ˈten.dər/		nounsoft and easy to cut or chewThe meat was so tender it fell apart.
wholesome
/ˈhəʊl.səm/		noungood for your healthShe cooks wholesome meals for her family.
ration
/ˈræʃ.ən/		nouna fixed amount of food allowedDuring the war, food was strictly rationed.
palate
/ˈpæl.ət/		nouna person's ability to taste and enjoy foodHe has a very refined palate.
