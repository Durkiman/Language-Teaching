📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
gastronomy
/ɡæsˈtrɒn.ə.mi/		nounthe art and practice of good eatingThe region is famous for its gastronomy.
delicacy
/ˈdel.ɪ.kə.si/		nouna rare or expensive food, considered specialCaviar is considered a delicacy.
culinary
/ˈkʌl.ɪ.nər.i/		nounrelating to cookingShe studied at a culinary school in Paris.
palatable
/ˈpæl.ə.tə.bəl/		nounpleasant to tasteThe dish was surprisingly palatable.
rancid
/ˈræn.sɪd/		nounhaving a bad smell or taste from ageThe butter had gone rancid.
sustenance
/ˈsʌs.tɪ.nəns/		nounfood and drink needed to liveThe travellers needed sustenance after the long hike.
fermentation
/ˌfɜː.menˈteɪ.ʃən/		nouna chemical process that changes food using bacteria or yeastFermentation gives yoghurt its sour taste.
umami
/uːˈmɑː.mi/		nouna savoury, meaty taste, one of the five basic tastesMushrooms add a rich umami flavour.
satiate
/ˈseɪ.ʃi.eɪt/		nounto satisfy hunger completelyOne bowl of pasta was enough to satiate him.
gluttony
/ˈɡlʌt.ən.i/		nouneating and drinking too muchThe feast was an act of pure gluttony.
austere
/ɒˈstɪər/		nounvery plain and simple, without luxuryTheir diet was austere during the crisis.
sumptuous
/ˈsʌmp.tʃu.əs/		noungrand, expensive-looking and impressiveThey enjoyed a sumptuous banquet.
morsel
/ˈmɔː.səl/		nouna small piece of foodShe offered him a morsel of cake.
connoisseur
/ˌkɒn.əˈsɜːr/		nounan expert who appreciates something, like food or wineHe's a connoisseur of fine wine.
indigenous
/ɪnˈdɪdʒ.ɪ.nəs/		nounnative to a particular placeQuinoa is indigenous to the Andes.
epicure
/ˈep.ɪ.kjʊər/		nouna person who enjoys and knows a lot about good foodHe was known as an epicure with refined taste.
