📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
apple
/ˈæp.əl/		nouna round fruit, often red or greenI eat an apple every day.
bread
/bred/		nouna food made from baked flour and waterShe buys fresh bread every morning.
cheese
/tʃiːz/		nouna solid food made from milkCan I have some cheese on my sandwich?
chicken
/ˈtʃɪk.ɪn/		nounmeat from a chickenWe had chicken and rice for dinner.
coffee
/ˈkɒf.i/		nouna hot drink made from roasted beansHe drinks coffee every morning.
egg
/eɡ/		nounan oval food laid by a henI usually eat an egg for breakfast.
fish
/fɪʃ/		nounan animal that lives in water, eaten as foodThey eat fish on Fridays.
juice
/dʒuːs/		nouna drink made from fruitWould you like some orange juice?
meat
/miːt/		nounfood from an animal's bodyShe doesn't eat meat.
milk
/mɪlk/		nouna white drink from cowsDo you take milk in your tea?
rice
/raɪs/		nounsmall white or brown grains, cooked as foodWe cook rice almost every day.
salad
/ˈsæl.əd/		nouna cold mix of vegetablesI'll have a salad, please.
soup
/suːp/		nouna liquid food often made with vegetables or meatThis soup is very hot.
tea
/tiː/		nouna hot drink made from leaves and hot waterShe always drinks tea in the afternoon.
vegetable
/ˈvedʒ.tə.bəl/		nouna plant food, like a carrot or a potatoCarrots are my favourite vegetable.
water
/ˈwɔː.tər/		nouna clear liquid we drinkCan I have a glass of water?
