📖 1. Word Bank

Word Type Meaning Example
applenouna round fruit, often red or greenI eat an apple every day.
breadnouna food made from baked flour and waterShe buys fresh bread every morning.
cheesenouna solid food made from milkCan I have some cheese on my sandwich?
chickennounmeat from a chickenWe had chicken and rice for dinner.
coffeenouna hot drink made from roasted beansHe drinks coffee every morning.
eggnounan oval food laid by a henI usually eat an egg for breakfast.
fishnounan animal that lives in water, eaten as foodThey eat fish on Fridays.
juicenouna drink made from fruitWould you like some orange juice?
meatnounfood from an animal's bodyShe doesn't eat meat.
milknouna white drink from cowsDo you take milk in your tea?
ricenounsmall white or brown grains, cooked as foodWe cook rice almost every day.
saladnouna cold mix of vegetablesI'll have a salad, please.
soupnouna liquid food often made with vegetables or meatThis soup is very hot.
teanouna hot drink made from leaves and hot waterShe always drinks tea in the afternoon.
vegetablenouna plant food, like a carrot or a potatoCarrots are my favourite vegetable.
waternouna clear liquid we drinkCan I have a glass of water?
