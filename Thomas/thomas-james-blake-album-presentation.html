James Blake

Who is he?

Read through, then we'll talk about it. The highlighted chunks are phrasal verbs worth reusing later.

James Blake is an English singer, producer and songwriter from London. He first came up in the early 2010s, mixing minimal electronic production, dubstep-influenced bass, and soulful, often fragile vocals. His sound is hard to pin down — it draws on soul, gospel, and electronic music at once, and critics still struggle to sum up exactly what genre he belongs to.

Blake's albums usually build up slowly rather than hitting you immediately — the production is spacious, sometimes almost silent, and the emotion tends to creep in gradually rather than all at once. He's known for close collaborations with other artists too; over the years he has teamed up with names like Frank Ocean, Travis Scott, and Bon Iver.

One thing that stands out about his music is how personal it feels — many of his songs deal with anxiety, heartbreak, and self-doubt, and he doesn't shy away from sounding vulnerable. That honesty is a big part of why fans keep coming back to his records.
