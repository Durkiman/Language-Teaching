2. What to send the student

Send both prompts together with these steps — this is the whole homework instruction.
  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer and switch on Voice Mode (the headphones/soundwave icon).
  2. Paste the first prompt below and speak your reply out loud — don't type.
  3. Keep the conversation going for the full time. If you don't understand something, ask it to repeat or explain in simpler words — that's allowed.
  4. When you're done, paste the second prompt to get quick feedback on how you did.
  5. Bring anything that felt hard to our next lesson.
