A city guide: Barcelona

Un guide de la ville : Barcelone

Barcelona is a big city in the north-east of Spain, next to the Mediterranean Sea. Tourists visit it for its architecture, its beaches and its food. Here are four places you can visit.

⛪

La Sagrada Família

This enormous church is the most famous building in Barcelona. The architect Antoni Gaudí started building it in 1882, and it still isn't finished! Tourists visit it to see the strange, colourful towers.

🌳

Park Güell

Park Güell is a colourful park with amazing views of the city. Gaudí designed it too. People go there to walk, take photos and relax with a view of the sea.

🥘

La Rambla & tapas

La Rambla is a long, busy street in the centre of the city, full of shops and cafés. In the evening, people go to small restaurants to eat tapas — lots of small dishes to share with friends.

🏖️

Barceloneta Beach

Barcelona has its own beach, right next to the city centre! In summer, people go there to swim, play volleyball and eat fresh fish in the restaurants by the sea.
