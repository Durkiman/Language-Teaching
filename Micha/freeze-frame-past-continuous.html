One Moment, Two Sentences

The exact same moment in a story can be described in two different ways — and both are correct. It depends what you want to focus on: the ongoing action, or the sudden event.

Le même moment exact dans une histoire peut être décrit de deux façons différentes — et les deux sont correctes. Ça dépend de ce sur quoi tu veux te concentrer : l'action en cours, ou l'événement soudain.

Goku was flying away when Freeza attacked him.
Freeza attacked him while Goku was flying away.

Ces deux phrases décrivent exactement le même moment — juste dans un ordre différent, avec "when" ou "while".

Past Continuous

Le passé continu — une action en cours

subject + was/were + verb-ing
SubjectFormExample
I / he / she / itwas + -ingGoku was flying away.
you / we / theywere + -ingThey were fighting for hours.

Past Continuous + Past Simple Together

Long action (continuous) + short interruption (simple):
"Goku was flying away when Freeza attacked him."
Same idea, different order, with "while":
"Freeza attacked him while Goku was flying away."
💡 Tip: The continuous action was already happening — the simple action was one sudden moment that cut into it. Meaning stays the same whether you use "when" or "while", or which clause comes first!

L'action continue était déjà en cours — l'action simple était un moment soudain qui l'a interrompue. Le sens reste le même avec "when" ou "while".

⚠️ Common mistakes:

✗ "Goku flying when Freeza attacked." → ✓ "Goku was flying..." (never forget was/were!)

✗ "Goku was flying when Freeza was attacking." — this changes the meaning — now both are ongoing, with no clear interruption.

✗ "Goku attacked when Freeza was flying." — check who is doing what! Read the sentence carefully.

N'oublie jamais was/were · Fais attention à qui fait quoi dans la phrase

Reading — The Battle

Lecture — La bataille

Goku was flying toward the enemy when Freeza suddenly appeared in front of him. While Goku was thinking about his next move, Freeza attacked without warning. Goku wasn't expecting it, but he dodged just in time. The two fighters were staring at each other when the real battle began.

Easy

1. Fill in the blank: Goku ___ ___ (fly) when Freeza attacked.

💡 Grammar tip
Background action → was/were + -ing → "was flying"
🇫🇷 French hint
Goku volait quand Freeza a attaqué.
Easy

2. Choose the correct sentence:

Freeza was attack Goku when he flying.
Freeza attacked Goku when he was flying.
Freeza attack Goku when he was fly.
🇫🇷 French hint
Action soudaine = passé simple. Action en cours = was + -ing.
Medium

3. Rewrite using "while": "Goku was fighting when Freeza laughed."

💡 Grammar tip
Same meaning, different order — the continuous action stays continuous!
🇫🇷 French hint
Freeza a ri pendant que Goku se battait.
Medium

4. Fill in both blanks: They ___ (stare) at each other when the fight ___ (begin).

 
💡 Grammar tip
First blank = ongoing → were staring. Second blank = sudden event → began.
Hard

5. Make it negative: "Goku was expecting the attack."

🇫🇷 French hint
Goku ne s'attendait pas à l'attaque. → "Goku wasn't expecting the attack."
Hard

6. Spot the mistake: "Freeza was attack when Goku was flying." Fix it.

💡 Grammar tip
Also check the meaning — should the attack be sudden (Past Simple) or ongoing?
Hard

7. Write your own "when" sentence about anything that happened to you.
