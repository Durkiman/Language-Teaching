My Home Country: Kosovo

There is / There are — Talking About Places

2. Today, we're going somewhere new — my home country! Where do you think I'm from?

Kosovo

Kosovo is a small country in the Balkans, in southeast Europe. It's surrounded by mountains, and it's the country I grew up in.

Le Kosovo est un petit pays des Balkans, dans le sud-est de l'Europe. Il est entouré de montagnes, et c'est le pays où j'ai grandi.

🏔️ My Hometown: Peja (Pejë)

I'm from a city called Peja, in the west of Kosovo. There is a huge mountain range right next to the city, called the Accursed Mountains — a dramatic name, but the views are incredible.

There is a famous canyon near Peja called Rugova Canyon. There are waterfalls, hiking trails, and even a rock-climbing route cut into the cliffs. There is also an old bazaar in the city centre, with narrow streets and small shops that have been there for generations.

Je viens d'une ville appelée Peja, dans l'ouest du Kosovo. Il y a une immense chaîne de montagnes juste à côté de la ville, appelée les Montagnes Maudites — un nom dramatique, mais la vue est incroyable. Il y a un canyon célèbre près de Peja appelé le Canyon de Rugova. Il y a des cascades, des sentiers de randonnée, et même une voie d'escalade taillée dans les falaises. Il y a aussi un vieux bazar au centre-ville, avec des rues étroites et de petites boutiques qui existent depuis des générations.

There is / There are

Il y a...

There is + singular noun  |  There are + plural noun
FormExample
There is (singular)There is a canyon near Peja.
There are (plural)There are many mountains in Kosovo.

"There is" et "There are" veulent tous les deux dire "Il y a" en français — mais en anglais, le verbe change selon si c'est singulier ou pluriel !

Negative & Question Forms

PositiveNegativeQuestion
There is a bazaar.There isn't a beach.Is there a bazaar?
There are mountains.There aren't any deserts.Are there mountains?
💡 Tip: For questions, just swap the order: "There is" → "Is there...?" / "There are" → "Are there...?"

Pour les questions, inverse simplement l'ordre !

⚠️ Common mistakes:

❌ "There is many mountains." → ✅ "There are many mountains." (plural noun needs "are")

❌ "There are a canyon." → ✅ "There is a canyon." (singular noun needs "is")

❌ "Is a bazaar there?" → ✅ "Is there a bazaar?" (word order for questions)

Nom pluriel → "are" · Nom singulier → "is" · Question : "Is/Are there...?"

Reading — A Trip to Peja

Lecture — Un voyage à Peja

There is a beautiful old bazaar in the centre of Peja. There are many small shops selling local food and crafts. Near the city, there is a huge canyon with a river running through it. There are waterfalls, hiking paths, and even mountain climbing routes. There isn't a beach in Kosovo — it's a landlocked country — but there is something better: some of the most beautiful mountains in the Balkans.

Easy

1. Fill in the blank: There ___ a famous canyon near Peja.

💡 Grammar tip
"canyon" is singular → "is"
🇫🇷 French hint
Il y a un canyon célèbre près de Peja.
Easy

2. Choose the correct sentence:

There is many mountains in Kosovo.
There are many mountains in Kosovo.
There mountains are in Kosovo.
🇫🇷 French hint
"mountains" est pluriel → "are"
Medium

3. Fill in: ___ ___ a beach in Kosovo? (make it a question)

💡 Grammar tip
"beach" is singular → "Is there...?"
🇫🇷 French hint
Y a-t-il une plage au Kosovo ? → "Is there a beach in Kosovo?"
Medium

4. Make it negative: "There are deserts in Kosovo."

🇫🇷 French hint
Il n'y a pas de déserts au Kosovo. → "There aren't any deserts in Kosovo."
Hard

5. Fill in both blanks: There ___ a bazaar in the city centre, and there ___ many old shops inside it.

 
💡 Grammar tip
"bazaar" = singular → is. "shops" = plural → are.
Hard

6. Write one true sentence about your own town using "There is" or "There are".

Speaking

À l'oral — pas de correction pendant, on note les erreurs pour la fin

Warm-up

Tell me 3 things there are in your own town or city.

Dis-moi 3 choses qu'il y a dans ta propre ville.

Core

Describe Peja to me as if I've never seen it — what is there? What isn't there?

Décris-moi Peja comme si je ne l'avais jamais vue — qu'est-ce qu'il y a ? Qu'est-ce qu'il n'y a pas ?

Challenge

Compare your town to Peja — what is there in both places, and what is different?

Compare ta ville à Peja — qu'est-ce qu'il y a dans les deux, et qu'est-ce qui est différent ?
