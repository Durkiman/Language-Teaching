Teacher note This is the A2-12 evidence task. Each side makes two suggestions using different exponents and rejects one politely, then the pair reaches a final consensus. Don't correct mid-roleplay — use the whiteboard stamps/notes to log errors for feedback at the end.

Plan the weekend outing

Organise la sortie du week-end

You and a friend need to agree on ONE thing to do this weekend. Look at the three options — you each make suggestions, accept or reject them politely, and agree on a final plan together.

How to play

Comment jouer

Step 1

Each person makes TWO suggestions using different phrases (Shall we…? / Let's… / Why don't we…? / Do you fancy…? / Would you like to…?).

Chaque personne fait DEUX suggestions avec des expressions différentes.

Step 2

React to each suggestion — accept some, but politely reject at least one.

Réagis à chaque suggestion — accepte-en certaines, mais refuse-en au moins une poliment.

Step 3

Agree on ONE final plan together and say when/where you'll meet.

Mettez-vous d'accord sur UN plan final et dites quand/où vous vous retrouverez.
