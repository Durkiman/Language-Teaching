Requests & offers

Demandes et offres

When you want something politely, or you want to help someone, use these:

Asking for something
Can I / Could I…?
Can I have a receipt? · Could I get a discount?
Asking someone to do something
Could you…? / Would you mind…?
Could you check the price? · Would you mind holding it for me?
Offering to help
Shall I…? / Would you like me to…?
Shall I wrap it for you? · Would you like me to send a confirmation email?
