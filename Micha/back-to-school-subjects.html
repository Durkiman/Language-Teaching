Teacher note A1-04 review — he's seen like/love/hate + verb-ing before (trip-prep lesson #18). This is quick recap, not first teaching. Move fast to the exercises.

like / love / hate + subject or verb-ing

aimer bien / adorer / détester + matière ou verbe en -ing

Subject + like/love/hate + noun
or
Subject + like/love/hate + verb-ing
  • I love Science. 🇫🇷 J'adore les sciences.
  • He likes doing experiments. 🇫🇷 Il aime bien faire des expériences.
  • They hate writing essays. 🇫🇷 Ils détestent écrire des dissertations.
  • She doesn't like Maths. 🇫🇷 Elle n'aime pas les maths.
⚠ Common mistake: Don't add "to" — say "I like studying", not "I like to studying".
