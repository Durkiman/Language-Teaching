Grammar Recap — There is / There are

Rappel — Il y a...

There is + singular noun  |  There are + plural noun
FormExample
There is (singular)There is a river in my city.
There are (plural)There are many old buildings.
NegativeThere isn't / There aren't
QuestionIs there...? / Are there...?

"canyon" = singulier → is · "mountains" = pluriel → are
