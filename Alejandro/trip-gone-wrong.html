Staying in the story when it gets interrupted

Fixed phrases for absorbing a question or twist mid-story without losing the thread — this is the actual target language for today, not grammar.

1Actually, that's exactly what happened next —
2Good question — let me think… yeah, so
3Where was I? Oh right —
4Well, if that had happened, I probably would have…
Drill these as recovery tools, not fillers — the point is that a good storyteller can take a real question ("how did you feel?"), a redirect question ("what if you'd missed it completely?"), or a genuine interruption and fold it back into the story instead of freezing or restarting from scratch. Model the contrast: someone who stalls / switches to Spanish vs. someone who says "good question — let me think" and keeps going.
