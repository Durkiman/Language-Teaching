Presentation · 10 min
The Right Mindset for 2026
Read the text carefully — out loud if possible. Tap highlighted words to see what they mean.
Reading Text
Every year, many people make new goals for their business. They write plans. They set targetstargets = specific goals you want to reach — objectifs. But there is something more important than a plan: mindset.

What is mindset? It is the way you think about problems. Successful entrepreneurs don't just have good ideas. They also know how to think when things go wrong.

Today, the business world is changing fastpresent continuous — happening right now, not finished. New technology like AI is changing how companies work. Prices are going up. Customers want different things than before. This makes many entrepreneurs feel stressed and confusedstressed = under pressure / confused = not understanding clearly.

But some entrepreneurs handle this wellhandle = deal with, manage a difficult situation. What do they do differently?

First, they stay curiouscurious = wanting to learn and ask questions. Instead of feeling afraid of change, they ask questions. They want to learn new things, even if it's difficult.

Second, they build good habitshabits = things you do regularly, almost automatically. They don't wait to feel motivated. They create routinesroutines = a fixed order of regular actions: they learn something new every week, they talk to their team often, and they take time to rest.

Third, they don't try to control everythingcontrol = have power over, decide what happens. They accept that some things are uncertainuncertain = not sure, not known — the opposite of certain. Instead of panicking, they make small decisions and adjustadjust = change slightly to fit a new situation when necessary.

Finally, successful entrepreneurs think about their purposepurpose = the main reason why you do something. Why did they start their business? What problem do they want to solve? When times are hard, this purpose helps them keep going.

Mindset is not something you're born with. It's something you practisepractise = do regularly to improve — same idea as practising a sport or instrument — every day, one decision at a time.
💡 Key idea: Mindset isn't fixed — it's a skill you build through curiosity, habits, flexibility, and purpose.
