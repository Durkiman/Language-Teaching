Story Practice — Narrative Retrieval

Interviewers often want a real moment, not a general answer. Tell each story with a shape: what happened → what you did → what you learned.
Let her tell the whole story before any correction. If tense slips happen, note them but don't interrupt — flag 1-2 patterns max at the end, not every slip. The goal is narrative flow, which is exactly what "tell me about a time when..." interview questions require.

Tell me about a time a patient or teacher surprised you.
Story
